# LRS4 Market Regime Intelligence — Roadmap

## 1. Purpose and ownership

Owner: LRS4 Market Regimes.

This roadmap tracks the controlled implementation, verification,
authorization, and research progression of Market Regime Intelligence.

LRS4 studies whether causally observable market regimes explain variation
in the behavior of an inherited, frozen LRS3 research candidate.

LRS4 must not redefine inherited LRS3 observation construction, eligibility,
ranking, timing, or other frozen upstream semantics.

This roadmap is a planning and execution-control document. It does not
authorize methodology changes, historical data access, outcome inspection,
production transfer, or autonomous AI operation.

## 3. Three-level execution tracking

ROADMAP: this permanent Markdown file defines objectives and major
checkpoints.

MAJOR CHECKLIST: every substantive development response displays all
major checkpoints for the active roadmap objective.

MINOR CHECKLIST: every substantive development response displays the
tasks required to complete the active major checkpoint.

Each executable command heading includes its STEP identifier.

Minor tasks require verified completion evidence. Major checkpoints
require completed minor tasks, acceptance criteria, and applicable audits.
Roadmap objectives require all their major checkpoints and objective-level
gates to pass.

Do not equate a test run, commit, push, or audit verdict with scientific
validity, profitability, or deployment authorization.

## 6. Independent Gemini audit workflow

At major decisions and applicable precommit gates, prepare an independent
audit packet using actual repository authority, proposed changes, diffs,
tests, and observed evidence.

Require one formal verdict:

`VERDICT: PASS`
`VERDICT: CHANGES REQUIRED`

On CHANGES REQUIRED, record each blocking finding as a minor task,
correct the issue, verify the correction, and request a fresh audit.
Do not advance or commit until the applicable audit passes.

Preserve the independent precommit audit and staged-diff review required
by `L0_PLATFORM/zHANDOFF.md`.

An audit PASS does not authorize historical research, methodology
amendment, production deployment, or autonomous operation.

## 7. Evidence and status discipline

Use these distinctions consistently:

VERIFIED: directly supported by current inspected evidence.
HISTORICAL: supported by dated records but not necessarily rerun.
PENDING: planned work without completion evidence.
BLOCKED: prerequisite or authorization missing.
NOT AUTHORIZED: action cannot begin without explicit owner permission.

Record source paths, relevant revisions, test commands and results,
audit verdicts, and blockers when marking checkpoints complete.

Never infer runtime health, experimental outcomes, or readiness from
the presence of source files.

## 8. Change control and handoff

Inspect before editing. Keep changes bounded. Preserve unrelated files,
untracked archives, frozen methodology, and sealed research boundaries.

Follow the repository's WRITE, VERIFY, DIFF, independent AUDIT,
remediation, fresh PASS, exact STAGE, staged DIFF, COMMIT, PUSH sequence.

A new AI or thread must inspect current repository authority and
reconstruct the active roadmap objective, major checkpoint, minor tasks,
blockers, and next authorized action before continuing.

The user runs every terminal command. Assistants do not directly edit
repository files, run commands, perform Git operations, or operate
infrastructure.
